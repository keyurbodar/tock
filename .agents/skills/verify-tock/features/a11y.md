# Accessibility

- `a11y` (journey): one visible `<main>` and a level-1 heading on home and in a chat; `document.title` is `Tock` on home and `<chat title> · Tock` in a chat; no viewport zoom lock; every visible control has an accessible name; every `<img>` has `alt`; nothing tabbable inside `aria-hidden`; no positive tabindex; every target is at least 24px (separators, inline links and text fields excepted); "Skip to chat" is the first Tab stop, shows on screen and moves focus to `#main`; up to 40 Tab stops through a seeded chat are visible and each shows a focus indicator (outline, the search box ring, the resize handle line, or the caret in the composer); a toast with focus inside it does not time out. Static rules live in `scripts/lint/a11y.ts`.
